## KEY LESSONS FROM THIS EXAMPLE

### What worked

1. **Specific cluster identity** — "Fur Baby Millennials with Senior Dogs" beat "Pet products" by miles. Allowed Brand Story to be vivid.

2. **Vertical CAC verification** — Cited Pet $23 CAC as critical insight. Without web search this number would be guess.

3. **White space discovery** — Through competitive research, found Spot & Tango ($100M ARR) doesn't have senior-specific formula. This single insight = entire positioning.

4. **Hook library specificity** — 60 hooks chia 10 categories beats "make some hooks". Team có thể test ngay không cần re-think.

5. **Phase economics breakdown** — Showing COGS evolution Phase 1→2→3 helps team plan capital allocation, not just current state.

### What to avoid (failure modes seen in earlier drafts)

1. ❌ Generic cluster: "Pet parents" — fixed by adding life moment
2. ❌ Single COGS number: "$10" — fixed by range "$8-12 from 100-MOQ Alibaba"  
3. ❌ No compliance check on supplements — fixed by adding Meta health-claims compliance section
4. ❌ Vague "trending" claim — fixed by specific search volume + YoY % data
5. ❌ Skipping founder story — fixed by drafting hook (essential for Meta ad performance)
