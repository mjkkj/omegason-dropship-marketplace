# Deep-Dive Playbook Template (MODE 2)

Khi user đã chọn 1 niche và yêu cầu deep-dive, build playbook theo cấu trúc 10 sections này. Output là file Markdown 600+ dòng, action-ready.

## Cấu trúc đầy đủ

```
# [NICHE NAME] — Deep-Dive Playbook
**Cluster:** [Vivid identity]
**Niche Score:** XX/100
**Working brand name (placeholder):** [Suggest 1]

## Navigation (table of contents)

## 1. Market Reality Check
## 2. Competitive Landscape  
## 3. White Space & Positioning
## 4. Supplier Research (3 Hero Products)
## 5. Product Strategy & Bundles
## 6. Brand Kit Framework
## 7. Ad Angles & Creative Playbook
## 8. Funnel & Unit Economics
## 9. Launch Roadmap (Dropship → OEM → Private Label)
## 10. Kill/Scale Decision Rules

## Appendix: Key Data Sources & Tools
```

## Section 1 — Market Reality Check

**Mục tiêu:** Verify ngách có demand thật, không phải hype.

**Required content:**
- Market size (TAM) với CAGR
- Trend signals (why "now"?)
- Search volume table cho 6-8 keywords (current month, 12mo trend)
- Adjacent markets/related categories sizing

**Web search queries:**
- "[niche] market size [year] US"
- "[main keyword] search volume Google Trends"
- "[niche] CAGR projection"

**Format example:**
```markdown
### Market Size
- US [niche] industry [year]: $XB → projected $YB by 2030
- Sub-segment ([specific]): $XB annually, CAGR X.X%

### Search & Demand Signals
| Keyword | Monthly Vol (US) | Trend (12mo) |
|---|---|---|
| [keyword 1] | XX,000 | ↗ +XX% |
```

## Section 2 — Competitive Landscape

**Mục tiêu:** Map đối thủ thực tế, không bịa.

**Required content:**
- Direct competitors table (5-10 brands) với positioning + pricing + strength + weakness
- Adjacent brands (cùng customer, khác category)
- Critical competitive insights (3-5 insights actionable)

**Web search queries:**
- "[niche] DTC brand competitor [year]"
- "[main product] best brands review [year]"
- "[niche] TikTok viral [year]"

**Format example:**
```markdown
| Brand | Positioning | Pricing | Strength | Weakness |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Brand A | Modern wellness | $34-48 | Branding, IG | Generic to all |
```

**Key insights to surface:**
1. White space (gap không ai own)
2. Pricing tier opportunities
3. Common ad angle weakness across competitors
4. Supplier/distribution insight

## Section 3 — White Space & Positioning

**Mục tiêu:** Define brand positioning trong 1-2 paragraphs.

**Required content:**
- The Gap (1 paragraph identifying white space)
- Positioning Statement (template: "For X, [BRAND] is the only Y that Z. Unlike A, we B.")
- Brand Promise (1 sentence, memorable)
- 5 Brand Pillars (specific, not generic like "quality")

**Format example:**
```markdown
### The Gap
Most [vertical] brands speak to [generic audience]. **[Specific cluster]** is emotionally different — they're [specific state].

### Positioning Statement
> For [specific ICP], [BRAND] is the only [category] that [unique mechanism], so you can [specific outcome]. Unlike [competitors], we don't [generic action]. We [specific action].

### Brand Promise
> *"[1-sentence pitch under 12 words]"*

### 5 Brand Pillars
1. **[Pillar name].** [Specific behavior, not vague value]
2. ...
```

## Section 4 — Supplier Research (CRITICAL)

**Mục tiêu:** Verified suppliers với pricing thật, không bịa.

**KHÔNG BAO GIỜ skip web search ở section này.**

**Required content cho mỗi của 3 hero products:**
- Key specs cần specify khi sourcing (5-8 bullets)
- Verified suppliers table (4-7 suppliers) với location, MOQ, unit price, lead time, notes
- Phase economics breakdown (3 phases)
- Sample order strategy
- Risk matrix

**Web search queries:**
- "[product specific name] Alibaba supplier MOQ price"
- "[product] OEM private label manufacturer"
- "[product] AliExpress dropship price"

**Format example:**
```markdown
### 4.1 Hero Product #1: [Product Name]

**Key specs:**
- [Spec 1 với detail]
- [Spec 2]

**Verified Suppliers (Alibaba):**

| Supplier | Location | MOQ | Unit Price | Lead Time | Best For |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Supplier A | Province | 50 pcs | $XX-XX | 15-20 days | ⭐ Trial |

**Phase economics:**
- Phase 1 (Dropship): COGS $X-Y → Retail $Z → Margin XX%
- Phase 2 (OEM): COGS $X + $Y packaging → $Z total → Retail $W → Margin XX%
- Phase 3 (Private Label): COGS $X → Retail $Y → Margin XX%

**Sample order strategy:**
- Place sample order ($30-50) from top 3 suppliers BEFORE committing
- Test: [specific tests for this product type]
```

**Phase definitions:**
- **Phase 1 (Months 1-3, Dropship):** AliExpress, no inventory, accept lower margin
- **Phase 2 (Months 4-9, OEM):** Alibaba bulk MOQ 50-500, branded packaging
- **Phase 3 (Months 10+, Private Label):** Custom formulation, possibly USA-made for premium positioning

## Section 7 — Ad Angles & Creative Playbook

**Mục tiêu:** 6 winning angles + 50-60 hook library, ready for production.

**Required content:**
- 6 winning angles với hook + format + why it works + compliance note
- Hook library 50-60 hooks chia theo categories: pain-state / curiosity / transformation / identity / authority / comparison / time-anchor / gift-buyer / mistake-warning / subtle
- Creative production plan (Phase 1 vs Phase 2)
- Ad compliance checklist

**Web search queries:**
- "[niche] TikTok viral ad [year]"
- "[niche] Meta ad creative compliance [year]"
- "[product] before after ad pattern"

**Format example:**
```markdown
### Angle 1: "[Angle Name]" 🥇 (Best-performing)
**Hook:** *"[Specific hook copy]"*
**Format:** [UGC vertical / static carousel / talking head, duration]
**Setup:** [Specific scene description]
**Why it works:** [3-line explanation]
**Compliance copy:** [Safe phrasing examples]

### Hook Library (60 hooks for testing)

**Pain-state hooks (9):**
1. "[Hook]"
...
```

**Hook library format:** Numbered list, organized by emotional category, each hook 6-15 words.

**Compliance section critical for:**
- Supplements (Meta health claims)
- Beauty (FDA cosmetic claims)
- Wellness (FTC substantiation)
- Pet products (some states have rules)

## Section 8 — Funnel & Unit Economics

**Mục tiêu:** Math chứng minh ngách work, không chỉ "feel good".

**Required content:**
- Target KPIs table (CTR, CPM, CPC, CR, AOV, CAC, repeat rate, subscription %) với industry benchmark column
- Unit economics worked example (single SKU + bundle) với LTV:CAC math
- Funnel architecture (top/mid/bottom strategies)

**Format example:**
```markdown
### Target KPIs (Phase 1)

| Metric | Target | Benchmark [vertical] [year] |
|---|---|---|
| CTR (cold ads) | ≥ 1.5% | 1.2% avg |
| CPM | ≤ $20 | $14-25 |
| CAC (cold) | ≤ $30 | $23 pet vertical avg |

### Unit Economics (Mature Phase, Month 6+)

**Single supplement bottle ($39 retail, $33 subscribe):**
- COGS: $X
- Shipping: $X
- Payment processing: $X
- CAC blended: $X
- = **Contribution margin Order 1: $X (X% margin)**
- Order 2-12 (subscribe): $X contribution per order
- 6-month subscriber LTV: $X contribution
- **LTV:CAC ratio = X.X:1** ✅ (target 3:1)
```

**Funnel components to address:**
- TOFU: Cold ads, organic content, Pinterest, SEO
- MOFU: Email opt-in lead magnet, retargeting, quiz
- BOFU: Bundle landing page, trial offer, post-purchase upsell

## Section 9 — Launch Roadmap

**Mục tiêu:** 3-phase timeline với milestones cụ thể.

**Required content:**
- Phase 1 (Months 1-3): Dropship Validation — goals, actions checklist, spend budget, success threshold
- Phase 2 (Months 4-9): OEM — goals, actions, success threshold to advance
- Phase 3 (Months 10+): Private Label — long-term moat-building

**Format example:**
```markdown
### Month 1-3: DROPSHIP VALIDATION

**Goals:**
- Validate demand & messaging
- Collect first 100-300 orders
- Build initial UGC library

**Actions:**
- [ ] Week 1: Build Shopify store, brand kit applied
- [ ] Week 2: Source 3 hero products via AliExpress
- [ ] Week 3: Produce first 10 ad creatives (5 angles × 2 formats)
- [ ] Week 4: Launch $300/day Meta Ads, 5 ad sets ABO testing

**Spend budget:** $X-Y K
**Success threshold to advance to Phase 2:**
- 100+ paying orders
- ROAS ≥ 2.0 across blended
- Repeat rate ≥ 20% in 60 days
- Clear winning hero SKU identified
```

## Section 10 — Kill/Scale Decision Rules

**Mục tiêu:** Pre-define decision rules để team không emotional về sunk cost.

**Required content:**
- KILL criteria (any 2 within first 60 days = pivot or kill)
- SCALE criteria (all met = double ad budget)
- PIVOT triggers (same cluster, different sub-niche)

**Format example:**
```markdown
### KILL Criteria (any 2 within first 60 days)
- ROAS sustained below 1.5 for 14 days after creative refreshes
- CTR cold under 0.8% across 8+ creatives tested
- Repeat rate < 15% at 60-day mark
- CAC > $X with no path below
- Refund rate > 8% (product quality issue)

### SCALE Criteria (all met = double budget)
- ROAS ≥ 2.5 sustained 14 days
- CTR cold ≥ 1.5% on top creatives
- ...

### PIVOT Triggers (same cluster, different sub-niche)
- Customer interviews show product-market fit issue but cluster love
- Supply chain breakdown on hero SKU
- Competitor launches direct copycat at lower price
```
