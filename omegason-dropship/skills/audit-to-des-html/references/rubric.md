# Rubric — 100 points

Same rubric used across all PDP audits. Keeps scores comparable over time.

| Section | Weight |
|---|---|
| First impression (3s test) | 15 |
| Hero & offer clarity | 20 |
| Trust signals | 15 |
| Product structure & info depth | 15 |
| CTA & checkout friction | 15 |
| Social proof consistency | 10 |
| Cross-sell / AOV | 10 |

## Verdict thresholds

- **80+** — ready to spend ad budget
- **65–79** — fix top 3 first, then scale
- **50–64** — major fixes, test small only
- **<50** — do not run paid traffic yet

## Scoring hints

**First impression** drops for: marquee banner loop spam, broken pricing math, currency mismatch (USD threshold + VND prices), AI-generated stock photo hero.

**Hero & offer clarity** drops for: headline = product name only, no outcome promise, price not visible above fold, bundle without "what's inside" surfacing.

**Trust signals** scores best with: discreet packaging (intimates), 30-day return, customer count claim with source, press logos, founder photo, third-party badges relevant to category.

**Product structure** wants: bullets > prose, spec table, size chart, demo media, ingredient/material list, clear "for whom / for what" block.

**CTA & checkout friction** rewards: sticky ATC mobile, Shop Pay/PayPal/AfterPay, delivery date estimate, single-step variant selectors, no forced account creation.

**Social proof** penalizes: cross-product reviews in single-PDP widget, multiple reviews same day with template wording, photos all magazine-quality (no UGC), star average without count.

**Cross-sell / AOV** rewards: frequently-bought-together, bonus content (e-book, guide), bundle discount visible, recently-viewed.
