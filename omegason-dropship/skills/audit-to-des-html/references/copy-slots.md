# Copy slot rules

Every `{{PLACEHOLDER}}` in the template has a written rule. Follow them.

## {{BRAND_MARK}}
Small italic line at very top of cover. Format: `<Brand> · Est. for <one outcome>`.
Example: "Lacy Nighty · Est. for unforgettable nights"

## {{COLLECTION_EYEBROW}}
ALL CAPS micro-text above main headline. Format: `The <Product Name>` or category name.
Max 4 words.

## {{DISPLAY_HEADLINE}}
The biggest type on the page. Must promise an outcome, not describe a feature.
Pattern that works: **`<Number>. <Outcome with italic accent>`**
- "Five pieces. One night she'll *remember for years.*"
- "Three steps. One face that *thanks you back.*"
- "Twelve sessions. One body that *moves like before.*"

The italic span goes inside `<em>` tags.

## {{LEAD_TAGLINE}}
One sentence under the rule, italic serif. Anti-objection or anti-hesitation.
Max 22 words. Format: "Designed for <who>, without the <friction>."
- "Designed for couples who want more than lace — without the awkward shopping trip."
- "Made for skin that's tried everything — without another 12-step routine."

## {{HERO_IMG_URL}} / {{HERO_IMG_ALT}} / {{HERO_CAPTION}}
- URL from Phase 2 image extraction
- ALT: descriptive but brand-safe
- CAPTION: 4–8 words italic, lifestyle not feature. Avoid "Shop now" / sell language.

## {{INTRO_HEADLINE}}
Pull-quote style. The line the audit identified as the missing emotional hook.

## {{INTRO_PARA}}
2–3 sentences. Says what this product replaces / improves / removes from the buyer's life. No feature listing.

## {{INSIDE_FEATURE_IMG_URL}}
Use a flat-lay or full-collection shot if available.

## {{PIECE_N}} — repeated for each piece
For bundles: one card per item.
For single product: rename section to "Why this piece" and use 3 cards for the 3 strongest value props.

Each piece needs:
- **Number** (No. 01 — No. 02 — etc.)
- **Name** (serif, italic-ready)
- **Body**: one sentence that says what it does + one micro-detail that proves care
  - Good: "Vegan leather outside, satin lining inside. No pinching, no red marks the next morning."
  - Bad: "High-quality material with excellent comfort."

## {{QUOTE_TEXT}} / {{QUOTE_CITE}}
Pull from real reviews (verify, do not fabricate).
If reviews are not relevant to this product (cross-product Judge.me feed), pick a generic verified quote from the brand's homepage or another PDP.
Cite as: `— Verified buyer · ★ ★ ★ ★ ★`

## {{SPLIT_IMG_URL}} / {{SPLIT_HEADLINE}} / {{SPLIT_BODY}}
The "Comfort is the seduction" kind of line. Reframes a feature as an emotional benefit.
Body explains what they obsessed over so the buyer doesn't have to.

## {{SPEC_DESIGN_LIST}} / {{SPEC_MATERIALS_LIST}}
Bullet pairs `label → value`.
Value uses italic serif (handled by CSS).
Values must be specific numbers/materials, not adjectives.

## {{TRUST_BLOCK_1_2_3}}
The 3 strongest privacy/shipping promises. For intimates: unmarked box / discreet billing / quiet returns.
For supplements: third-party tested / refund / clean ingredients.
For gadgets: warranty / fast support / return.

## {{BONUS_TITLE}} / {{BONUS_DESC}} / {{BONUS_VALUE}}
If the product includes any bonus (e-book, sample, consult), surface it here.
If no bonus, replace this whole section with a "How to enjoy this" mini-guide instead.

## {{FAQ_Q1–Q6}}
Take directly from the audit's "Friction / blockers" list.
Each answer ≤4 sentences. Speak in brand voice, not customer service tone.

## {{CLOSING_LINE}} / {{SIG_LINE}}
- CLOSING: re-state the cover headline's emotional promise in a single italic sentence.
- SIG: `<Brand> · for <one core outcome>`
