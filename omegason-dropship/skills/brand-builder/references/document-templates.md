## 01 Brand Strategy

```
## 1. Brand Purpose — Why does this brand exist?
[The emotional "why" — not "to sell X" but the deeper need it fulfills]

## 2. Mission Statement
[One sentence: what the brand does, for whom, and the transformation it creates]

## 3. Vision Statement
[One sentence: where the brand is headed in 5-10 years]

## 4. Core Values (3-5)
[Each value gets: Name + 2-3 sentence explanation of what it means in practice]

## 5. Brand Positioning Statement
> For [target] who [need],
> [Brand] is a [category] that [key differentiator].
> Unlike [competitor 1], [competitor 2], and [competitor 3],
> [Brand] [unique value proposition].
> We believe [reason to believe / brand philosophy].

## 6. Competitive Positioning Map
[Text-based 2×2 grid showing where the brand sits vs. competitors]
[Axes should reflect the TWO most strategically relevant dimensions]

## 7. Customer Personas
[Approach Logic note explaining persona priority]
### 7.1. [Priority Persona]
  - Demographics table
  - Psychographics (4-5 bullet points)
  - Pain Points (4-5 quotes in first person)
  - Purchase Triggers
  - Discovery Channels
### 7.2. [Secondary Persona]
  [Same structure]

## 8. Price Architecture
[Table: Tier | Price Range | Products | % of Collection | Purpose]
[Include rationale for floor, sweet spot, ceiling, and AOV target]

## 9. Brand Architecture
[Collection/product line structure — text-based tree diagram]

## 10. Brand Promise
[Per-persona promises: what the brand guarantees to each customer type]

## 11. Strategic Differentiators
[Table: 5 things that make this brand different, with how each manifests]

## 12. Success Metrics
[Table: Key metrics with 9-month targets]
```
