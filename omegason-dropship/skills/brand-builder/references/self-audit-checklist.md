# Self-Audit Checklist

Run this checklist after completing all 9 documents. For each item, verify against the original Core Work & Target Outcomes and the chosen strategic approach.

---

## Audit Matrix

For EACH of the 9 files, check:

### 1. Approach Alignment
- Does this document reflect the CHOSEN approach?
- If the approach says "lead with [Persona X]," is that persona given priority in THIS document?
- Are the approach's unique differentiators prominent (not buried)?
- Does the tone/framing match the approach's narrative?

### 2. Completeness
- Does the document contain every section listed in the plan?
- Are there any placeholder sections or TODO markers?
- Are all tables filled with actual data (not "TBD")?

### 3. Internal Consistency
- Do persona names match across all 9 files?
- Do budget numbers add up to the total monthly budget?
- Do revenue projections in file 05 match assumptions in files 06-08?
- Do content pillar percentages in file 04 match priority emphasis in file 06 (ads)?
- Do team assignments in file 09 match workload implied by files 04-08?

### 4. Specificity
- Could a team execute this tomorrow without asking clarifying questions?
- Are there specific numbers (not "increase engagement" but "reach 3% engagement rate")?
- Are timelines specific (not "later" but "Month 4, Week 2")?
- Are creative concepts described enough to brief a designer/copywriter?

### 5. Brand Uniqueness
- If you replaced the brand name with "ACME," would the document still make sense for a completely different brand? If yes, it's too generic.
- Does the document reference the specific market research findings?
- Are competitor references specific to THIS brand's competitive set?
- Do customer persona quotes reflect THIS customer's actual language?

---

## Common Failure Patterns

### Pattern 1: Persona Priority Drift
**Symptom:** Approach says "lead with Him/gift buyers" but file 01 lists "Her" as Primary Persona, file 04 allocates only 20% to gift content, and file 06 gives 70% budget to Her audiences.
**Fix:** Update persona labels, content split, and ad budget to reflect the chosen approach's priority sequence.

### Pattern 2: Hero Product Buried
**Symptom:** The brand's key differentiator (e.g., a special box, a subscription, a customization tool) is mentioned once in file 01 and then barely appears in files 04-09.
**Fix:** Give the hero product its own section in files 05 and 09, feature it prominently in ad concepts (file 06), and ensure content strategy (file 04) dedicates significant pillar space to it.

### Pattern 3: Known Risks Unaddressed
**Symptom:** The approach was selected WITH acknowledged weaknesses (e.g., seasonal dependency, narrow market), but the GTM plan doesn't include mitigation strategies.
**Fix:** Add a dedicated risk mitigation section to file 05 with specific tactics to counter each known weakness.

### Pattern 4: Budget Math Breaks
**Symptom:** Monthly allocations don't sum to the stated budget. Or revenue projections assume ROAS that contradicts ad spend in file 06.
**Fix:** Cross-check all financial tables. Create a simple formula: Ad Spend × ROAS = Ad Revenue. Email Revenue = X% of total. Organic = Y%. Sum should equal total revenue projection.

### Pattern 5: Generic Content
**Symptom:** Content pillars, ad concepts, and email flows could apply to any brand in the category. No reference to the brand's unique positioning, market research insights, or customer language.
**Fix:** Revisit research findings and inject brand-specific details into every document. Use actual keyword data, actual competitor names, actual customer quotes.
